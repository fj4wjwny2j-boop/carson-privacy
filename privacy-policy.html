Carson

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Carson collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your home profile, vehicles, maintenance items, categories, service history, and settings are stored only on your device (and in your device backups, which you control). We have no server and no way to see any of it.

Network connections

Carson works fully offline. It makes no network requests — it has no server, no third-party SDKs, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing you enter ever leaves your device.

Notifications

Carson can send reminder notifications about upcoming or overdue maintenance. These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device by iOS. No notification content is sent anywhere. You can grant or revoke notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Carson collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date. Since the app collects nothing, we expect little to change.
