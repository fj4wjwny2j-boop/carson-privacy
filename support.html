Getting started

When you first open Carson, it walks you through a short setup — a few questions about your home and any vehicles — then builds a tailored checklist of the maintenance that matters. Add, edit, or complete items any time from the main screens.

Reminders

Carson sends local notifications before tasks come due. If you're not seeing them, check that notifications are enabled for Carson in iOS Settings → Notifications → Carson.

Your data

Everything lives on your device. Carson has no account and no server, so your information is included in your normal device/iCloud device backups and restores with them. Deleting the app removes its data.
